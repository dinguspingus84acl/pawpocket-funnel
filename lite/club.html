The objections, answered

Is the $19.99/mo a recurring charge?

Yes, if you choose VIP. Today's $29.99 covers the kit and your first 30 days. After that it's $19.99/mo on the date shown at checkout, billed as PAWPOCKETCLUB*MEMBER, until you cancel in 1 click. Guest is $49.99 once and never renews.

Is this insurance?

No. PawPocket Digital Club is a wellness membership. It does not pay claims and it is not a reimbursement fund.

Do I get a live veterinarian or a clinic discount?

No. This Fast-Launch edition does not include live human veterinarians or clinic discounts. The helper is a 24/7 AI Pet Symptom & Toxic-Food Educational Helper (AI educational guidance only — not a licensed veterinarian, diagnosis, or emergency replacement).

Does the collar tag use GPS?

No. It is a battery-free QR/NFC smart collar tag. Any smartphone camera opens the pet's emergency contact and med profile. There is no GPS radio in the tag.

How do I cancel?

One click at cancel.html. The Cancel Membership Immediately button stays visible on every step.
