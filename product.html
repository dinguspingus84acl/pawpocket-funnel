Questions, answered

What does $49.99 buy?

The Calm & Safe Kit, once. A steam deshedding brush, a suction lick mat, and a battery-free QR/NFC collar tag. Nothing renews unless you check the optional VIP box.

What if I check the VIP box?

Only if you check Add PawPocket VIP Club. The club is $0.00 today, then $29.99/mo on the date in the terms, billed as PAWPOCKETCLUB*MEMBER, until you cancel in 1 click. Leave the box empty and the kit is $49.99 once, with nothing renewing. Checking it also changes today's kit to the VIP price of $29.99.

What if I want the kit back?

[CONFIRM GUARANTEE TERMS] Use the brush, the mat and the tag for 30 days. If the kit isn't worth it, send it back for a full refund of today's kit price. Club months already used, if you added VIP, are billed separately, and you can cancel that add-on in 1 click any time.

Is this insurance?

No. PawPocket Club is a wellness membership. It is not insurance and it does not pay claims. It does not pay claims and it is not a reimbursement fund.

Will a veterinarian prescribe online?

No. If you add VIP, veterinary access is 24/7 licensed veterinary guidance, triage, and second opinions. It is not an online prescription service and it is not a legal diagnosis where a state veterinary-client-patient relationship bars that.

Does the collar tag use GPS?

No. It is a battery-free QR/NFC smart collar tag. Any smartphone camera opens the pet's emergency contact and med profile. There is no GPS radio and no tracking subscription in the tag.

How do I cancel VIP?

One click at cancel.html. Cancel Membership Immediately stays on that screen. You only need it if you checked the add-on.

What actually ships?

The physical kit ships once. If you add VIP, renewals are the optional club, so shipping on those renewals is $0.
