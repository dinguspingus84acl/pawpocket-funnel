Compliance checklist

These rules are baked into both funnels. The empty story slots stay empty until a real customer says yes.

  • No fabricated member counts, star ratings, press logos, or testimonials. Story blocks say [INSERT REAL CUSTOMER STORY] or [INSERT REAL REVIEW BEFORE LAUNCH].
  • No countdown timers and no fake scarcity.
  • PawPocket Club is a wellness membership. It is not insurance and it does not pay claims. There is no self-funded emergency reimbursement pool.
  • The collar tag is battery-free QR/NFC. Any smartphone camera opens the emergency contact and med profile. No GPS claim.
  • Version A vet access is worded only as 24/7 licensed veterinary guidance, triage, and second opinions. No online prescriptions. No diagnosis where a state VCPR bars it.
  • Version A savings are a veterinary discount plan at participating clinics. Not insurance.
  • Version B never claims a live veterinarian or a clinic discount. The helper is AI educational guidance only.
  • The kit is $49.99. The optional VIP checkbox starts unchecked. Its terms name $0.00 today, the monthly price, the day-30 date, PAWPOCKETCLUB*MEMBER, and the cancel page.
  • Cancel Membership Immediately stays visible on every cancel screen. Pause, downgrade, and annual are optional and sit beside it.
